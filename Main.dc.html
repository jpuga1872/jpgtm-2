Three familiar ways to open Europe, and a fourth.

Hire a country manager

A full executive salary, a recruitment cycle and a legal entity, all committed before you know whether the market responds.

Sign a generalist distributor

Quick to announce, slow to produce. Your product becomes one line in a long portfolio, sold by people who never studied its fit with the market.

Sell from headquarters

No extra cost and little traction. Regulated buyers in Zurich, Madrid or Frankfurt expect a vendor who knows their rules and someone they can meet.

A fractional growth team in Europe

A senior lead and the specialists your case needs, for as much time as the work requires. A defined scope, a clear decision point and nothing to unwind if the answer is no.

Approach

Three stages, each ending in a decision you can defend to your board.

01

Explore

A market read built on evidence. Which countries, segments and accounts fit, who already owns the relationship, what European regulation and procurement demand, and which partners can carry you in.

You leave with: a go or no-go recommendation, a target account list and an entry plan.

02

Open

I represent you in the market. Positioning and materials adapted for European buyers, account-based outreach to named institutions, partner introductions, and first meetings that I attend with your team.

You leave with: qualified opportunities and an honest view of what buyers said.

03

Build

Once pipeline justifies it, I design the permanent set-up. The coverage model, the first sales and business development hires, the partner agreements, and a playbook your team can run without me.

You leave with: a European operation that stands on its own.
